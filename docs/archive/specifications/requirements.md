## Scope and design coverage

All 50 numbered UI, FEED, CON, REV, ADM, L10N and AGT requirements and all 12 SEC requirements are represented individually. Nine NFR categories, seven data contracts/rules, eight operational instructions and the contact field contract cover unnumbered source tables and deployment instructions. Source assumptions A-01–07 and questions Q-01–10 are discussed in the design and open-question register. Source workflows are covered by the related acceptance journeys. Excluded publishing/social/payment/multi-tenant/automatic-translation capabilities remain excluded. Carousel UI and public review submission are not initial requirements.
